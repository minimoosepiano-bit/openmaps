    OpenMaps — how to play

    Draw history the way you want it. Paint nations, shape coastlines, design flags, then push armies across the map and watch borders move. Everything you do is recorded to a timelapse you can export as a video.

    • Paint / Erase / Fill – pixel-perfect territory brush. Pick a nation on the right first.
    • Land / Water – reshape the terrain.
    • Arrow – drag anywhere on the map to draw an arrow for the selected nation. A front advances along it and the land it crosses changes hands. The brush size sets how wide the arrow's swath is. Curves are fine.
    • Unit – click to place an army for the selected nation, then drag from the army to draw the arrow it should follow.
    • The army marches along its arrow and every bit of land it crosses becomes its nation's. When two enemy armies meet they stop and fight; the bigger, healthier army wins and continues. Adjust size / reinforce to decide the outcome.
    • PAUSE – freeze time to draw several arrows first, then let them all go at once.
    • Timeline – scrub through history, play it back, or EXPORT VIDEO (WebM, no external software needed).
    • Camera – scroll to zoom, hold Space / middle-mouse / Pan tool to drag.
    • Shortcuts – B E G I L W U V H tools · [ ] brush size · Ctrl+Z undo · Space play/pause · Esc cancels an arrow · Delete removes unit.
